    Built around one need: keep up with every word.

    Live captions listen to the presenter's microphone and transcribe speech in real time, so a deaf or hard-of-hearing student never has to lip-read across a room, and a mute presenter can type or pre-record narration to accompany their slides.

    • Large, high-contrast caption text with adjustable size
    • English and Tamil speech recognition
    • A scrollback transcript panel to re-read anything missed
    • Full keyboard control — no mouse required to navigate slides

    Browser note: live captions use your browser's built-in speech recognition, which currently works best in Chrome and Edge. The presentation and slide viewer work everywhere.
